1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & FEATURE AUDIT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Candidate Promotional Features Tested:
  • emailer_and_homepage (binary interaction: both channels active)
  • has_any_promo (binary indicator: at least one channel active)
  • email_discount_interaction (emailer * discount_percent)
  • homepage_discount_interaction (homepage * discount_percent)
  • promo_discount_interaction (dual promo * discount_percent)
  • discount_percent_squared (quadratic price elasticity)
  • prev_promo_orders (strictly past order volume of previous campaign for meal-center)
  • rolling_promo_mean (strictly past historical promotional average)
  • rolling_promo_max (strictly past maximum promotional peak)
  • count_prior_promos (number of past promotional campaigns prior to target week)
  • hist_promo_uplift (ratio of past promo mean to baseline rolling demand)
